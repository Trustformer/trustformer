- **[perf] `Opaque` demotes a constant in the KERNEL conversion oracle, not just in tactics.** `may_write` was left `Local Opaque` while `Qed` type-checked `may_write0_latest_write0_None`; the kernel then refused to delta-unfold it and normalised the whole `log_existsb`/`log_app`/`getenv`/`ContextEnv` tower instead. Moving `Local Opaque may_write.` to AFTER `Qed.` (proof text unchanged) took the lemma from ~70 s to 0.007 s. Rule: never leave a constant `Opaque` at `Qed` time if the proof term's type-checking needs it unfolded — re-establish opacity after the `Qed`. — evidence: coq/Properties/Synthesis.v may_write0_latest_write0_None / may_write0_latest_write_None, A/B under `coqc -time`
- **[perf] A tactic-built `Defined` `FiniteType`/`FiniteType2` instance is a global build-time landmine.** `_reg_t_fin2` was one ~90-line tactic script closed with `Defined`, so every conversion that went through `Log R ContextEnv` had to delta-unfold `_reg_t_finite -> FiniteType2_FiniteType -> <giant record>` and re-normalise it: ~10 s per downstream `Qed`. Fix: split the DATA fields into plain `Definition`s (`_reg_t_index2`, `_reg_t_elements2`) and the three PROOF fields into `Qed`-opaque lemmas, then assemble with a record literal. `latest_write0_input_fold_neq` went 10.03 s -> 0.061 s. A/B control: the same lemma with an abstract `Context (fin : FiniteType reg_t)` section variable (kernel cannot unfold it) was already 0.003 s, which is how the instance was fingered. — evidence: coq/TypedSynthesis.v _reg_t_fin2, bisect against an abstract FiniteType section variable
- **[perf] Diagnose Coq slowness with `coqc -time` + a char-offset→line mapper, not by guessing at tactics.** `coqc -time` prints `Chars A - B [sentence] T secs`; converting offsets to line numbers and attributing them to the enclosing lemma pinpointed 13 hot sentences in `Synthesis.v` immediately. Result: `sauto`/`hammer` calls were ALL ≈0.005 s — the entire 321 s was kernel conversion at `Qed`. Do not blame the automation before measuring. — evidence: coqc -time on coq/Properties/Synthesis.v, 321.3 s -> 10.4 s after two conversion fixes
- **[env] Build only via `nix develop --command bash --norc --noprofile -c '<cmd>'`.** Without `--norc --noprofile` the interactive rc re-activates conda and resets PATH, picking a foreign `coqc` and yielding "Compiled library Koika.Frontend makes inconsistent assumptions over library Coq.Init.Ltac".
